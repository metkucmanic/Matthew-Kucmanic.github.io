Research experience
======
**Graduate Research Assistant, University of Iowa, College of Law (2023–present)**

Advisor: Anya Prince

- Analyzed journal articles for key themes related to polygenic research on social traits
- Summarized interview transcripts through memos for thematic analysis
- Led focus groups of biobank participants
- Cleaned and analyzed survey data

This work resulted in four publications, two of which are in pre-print. This work was funded through a National Human Genome Research Institute (NHGRI) of the National Institutes of Health (NIH) R01 grant titled Beyond the Medical: The ELSI of Polygenic Scores for Social Traits (NIH NHGRI R01HG012402).
